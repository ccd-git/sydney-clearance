Magnetic Latching Relay 120A 12V — Bistable, Holds With Zero Coil Power

120A 250VAC · copper busbar M6 terminals · 43 × 33 × 22mm · 30 in stock

A$25 Bulk pricing available on ten or more

A real latching relay: it closes on a pulse, opens on a pulse, and then holds that state with the coil completely dead. That is the whole point. An ordinary relay has to keep its coil energised to stay closed — around 2 to 5 watts, running the whole time, which over a month pulls roughly 150Ah out of a 12V battery. Use an ordinary relay to stop something draining your battery and the relay itself becomes the drain. This one draws nothing to hold.

Built for 4WD dual-battery setups and boats. Cut the link to the start battery when you park up, so the fridge, dash cam, UHF and chargers cannot flatten it — and the isolator adds no drain of its own. On a boat it holds through a mooring without drawing current, and the contacts are held by a permanent magnet rather than spring tension, so engine vibration does not make them chatter.
